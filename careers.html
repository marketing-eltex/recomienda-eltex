Come work with us at Eltex

Eltex is building the technological infrastructure for residential electrification in Europe, one of the largest and least digitized markets within the energy transition.

We operate an integrated platform that connects digital acquisition, structured financing, operational execution, and long-term maintenance into one scalable system. By combining energy, software, data, and AI, we bring efficiency, operational visibility, and strong unit economics to a traditionally fragmented industry.

Our technology enables smarter operations, better decision-making, and scalable deployment of residential electrification.

Our ambition is clear: to become the reference platform for home electrification in Europe, building a company defined by disciplined growth, capital efficiency, and international expansion. ⚡
